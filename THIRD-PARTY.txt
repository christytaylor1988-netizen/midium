MIDIum uses original glyph-to-note conversion, synthesis and interface code.
It contains no sampled recordings and no proprietary font binaries.

Bundled dependencies (offline copies):

https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/
https://github.com/opensheetmusicdisplay/opensheetmusicdisplay/wiki/Getting-Started
The verification MusicXML schema is the W3C MusicXML 4.0 schema, with only
schema-import locations changed to local files for offline validation.
