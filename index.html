MIDIum scans real letterforms into horizontal notes: left to right is time, bottom to top is pitch. This is deliberately chromatic music; filled letters can be dense and dissonant.

Choose a typeface, then press PLAY. FONT SIZE changes the pitch range; TRANSPOSE moves it. TIME SCALE stretches the letters in musical time; TEMPO changes how quickly that time passes. Changing the instrument does not alter your notes.

Line breaks become a short gap before the next line on the timeline. Your original text is kept intact in TEXT view and in export metadata. Unsupported characters are flagged and stop conversion, rather than being silently replaced.

Installed Helvetica, Times New Roman and Comic Sans MS are used when available. Otherwise the clearly labelled, bundled OFL fonts Arimo, Tinos and Comic Neue provide substitutes. No proprietary font files are bundled.

Bold, Italic and Underline change the actual letterforms and exported notes. Sheet music and its PDF always use black ink on white paper.

Attack (1 ms–2 s), Release (10 ms–6 s), Reverb (0–100%), Tone, Length (0.5–60 seconds), Woozy Chorus (0–100%) and Pitch (−12 to +12 semitones) shape browser sound only. Short notes may release before a long attack reaches full volume. LOOP repeats the whole composition on the audio clock, carrying release and effect tails into the next pass. Turning LOOP off finishes the current pass. Natural endings let tails decay; STOP fades out notes and effect tails together. Pitch shifts the browser sound only; MIDI TRANSPOSE still moves the actual notes. Decay and sustain remain internally set to useful defaults. These sounds are not embedded in MIDI or MusicXML. All sound controls stay visible in each view. FIT PAGE shows a complete A4 page; zoom in to inspect notation, and use the arrows to turn pages.

The piano roll and MIDI share exactly the same note events. An adaptive time signature keeps dense bars manageable without changing note timings. Score notation divides sustained notes into tied chords across rhythmic boundaries; no pitches are discarded. Dense chromatic clusters may be difficult to read or impossible for a human to perform. PDF and score rendering are limited to 96 bars / 40,000 notation noteheads; MIDI and MusicXML remain available beyond that.

Limit: 2,000 characters, 24,000 raster columns and 60,000 note events. Large scores take time. Playback stops if the browser tab is hidden. Exported MIDI contains a General MIDI program; Logic’s chosen instrument determines its final timbre.
